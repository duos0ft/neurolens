# Employee Support Policy

DuoSoft LTD is a fictional company. This policy is synthetic and exists only for the NeuroLens MSc project.

## Reasonable adjustments

DuoSoft provides reasonable adjustments to help employees work comfortably and effectively.

Employees do not need a formal diagnosis to request an adjustment, and they do not need to disclose a medical condition or disability.

Examples of adjustments include written instructions, flexible start times, noise-cancelling headphones, quiet working spaces, and changes to how meetings are run.

## Requesting an adjustment

Employees can request an adjustment by speaking to their line manager or by contacting People Operations directly.

People Operations will meet the employee to discuss the request within ten working days.

Agreed adjustments are recorded in a workplace adjustment passport. The employee owns the passport and decides who it is shared with.

## Employee Assistance Programme

All employees can use the Employee Assistance Programme free of charge.

The Employee Assistance Programme provides confidential counselling, available 24 hours a day by phone.

Employees can receive up to six free counselling sessions per issue.

DuoSoft does not receive information about which employees use the Employee Assistance Programme.

## Wellbeing days

Employees receive two paid wellbeing days per leave year.

Wellbeing days can be taken at short notice by informing the line manager on the day. They do not need to be explained.

Unused wellbeing days cannot be carried over into the next leave year.

## Raising a concern

Employees who are unhappy with how an adjustment request has been handled can raise a concern with the Head of People Operations.
