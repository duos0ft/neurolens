# Data Handling Policy

DuoSoft LTD is a fictional company. This policy is synthetic and exists only for the NeuroLens MSc project.

## Data classification

DuoSoft classifies information into three levels: Public, Internal, and Confidential.

Public information may be shared with anyone. Internal information may be shared with DuoSoft employees and approved contractors. Confidential information may only be shared with people who need it for their work.

Customer data and employee personal data are always classified as Confidential.

## Storing and sharing information

Confidential information must only be stored in DuoSoft-approved systems.

Employees must not store Confidential information on personal devices or personal cloud storage accounts.

Confidential information must not be sent to external email addresses unless the recipient is approved and the attachment is encrypted.

## Using AI tools

Employees must not enter Confidential information into public AI tools or chatbots.

Only AI tools approved by Information Security may be used with Internal information.

## Reporting incidents

Employees must report a suspected data breach or lost device to Information Security within 24 hours of discovering it.

Reports are made by emailing security@duosoft.example or by calling the IT service desk.

Employees who report an incident in good faith will not face disciplinary action for making the report.

## Retention

Employees must not keep Confidential information for longer than it is needed for its business purpose.

Each team's data retention periods are listed in the retention schedule maintained by Information Security.
