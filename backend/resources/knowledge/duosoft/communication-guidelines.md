# Communication Guidelines

DuoSoft LTD is a fictional company. These guidelines are synthetic and exist only for the NeuroLens MSc project.

## Principles

DuoSoft expects workplace messages to be clear, direct, and respectful.

When asking a colleague to do something, state what is needed, who it is for, and when it is needed by.

Avoid relying on hints or implied requests. If a deadline matters, write the deadline explicitly.

## Response times

Colleagues should reply to direct chat messages within one working day.

Colleagues should reply to emails within two working days.

Messages marked as urgent should only be used for issues that need action on the same working day.

Employees are not expected to read or reply to work messages outside their working hours.

## Choosing a channel

Use team chat channels for quick questions and day-to-day coordination.

Use email for formal decisions, external communication, and anything that needs a written record.

Use a meeting or call when a topic needs discussion between several people or is likely to cause misunderstanding in writing.

## Meetings

Meeting invitations should include an agenda or a short description of the purpose of the meeting.

Employees may ask for meeting notes, written summaries, or agendas in advance as a standard working practice. These requests do not need to be justified.

Cameras are optional in internal video meetings.

## Asking for clarification

Any employee may ask for a request to be clarified or written down. Asking for clarification is encouraged and is not considered a performance concern.
