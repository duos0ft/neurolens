# Remote Work Policy

DuoSoft LTD is a fictional company. This policy is synthetic and exists only for the NeuroLens MSc project.

## Hybrid working pattern

DuoSoft operates a hybrid working model. Employees in hybrid roles may work remotely for up to three days per week.

Employees in hybrid roles are expected to work from the office at least two days per week. Each team agrees its shared office days with its line manager.

Employees in their probation period work from the office at least three days per week unless their line manager agrees otherwise.

## Requesting a different pattern

Employees who would like to work remotely more than three days per week must submit a flexible working request through the HR portal.

DuoSoft responds to flexible working requests within four weeks. Reasonable adjustments related to health or disability are handled through the Employee Support Policy and do not require a flexible working request.

## Working from another country

Employees must not work from outside the United Kingdom without written approval from People Operations.

Approved overseas remote work is limited to a maximum of 20 working days in any 12-month period.

## Equipment and expenses

DuoSoft provides a laptop, monitor, keyboard, and headset for remote work.

Employees in hybrid roles may claim a one-off home office allowance of up to £300 for furniture such as a desk or chair. Claims require a receipt and must be submitted within three months of starting a hybrid role.

DuoSoft does not reimburse home energy or internet costs.

## Availability

Remote employees are expected to be reachable during their team's core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 UK time, unless they are on leave or have agreed a different arrangement with their line manager.
