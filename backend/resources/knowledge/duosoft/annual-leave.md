# Annual Leave Policy

DuoSoft LTD is a fictional company. This policy is synthetic and exists only for the NeuroLens MSc project.

## Purpose

This policy explains how much paid annual leave DuoSoft employees receive, how to request it, and what happens to unused leave.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per leave year, in addition to public holidays.

Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis, calculated from their contracted working hours.

After five years of continuous service, employees receive one additional day of annual leave per year, up to a maximum of 28 days.

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## Requesting leave

Employees must request annual leave through the HR portal before taking it.

Requests for five or more consecutive working days must be submitted at least four weeks in advance.

Requests for fewer than five consecutive working days must be submitted at least five working days in advance.

Line managers aim to approve or decline a request within three working days. A request is not approved until the employee receives confirmation in the HR portal.

## Carrying over leave

Employees may carry over up to five days of unused annual leave into the next leave year.

Carried-over leave must be used by 31 March of the following leave year. Any carried-over leave not used by that date is lost.

Leave that could not be taken because of sickness or parental leave is not subject to the five-day limit. Employees should contact People Operations to arrange this.

## Leave during notice periods

Employees who are leaving DuoSoft are paid for any accrued annual leave that has not been taken by their final working day.

## Questions

Questions about annual leave should be sent to People Operations at people@duosoft.example.
